### Bug Fixes

* A ref action on a target that stays disabled now ends after 2 s with `ACTION_FAILED`, `details.kind: "target_disabled"`, and a suggestion that names the element as disabled. Before, it waited the full 5 s and returned a `TIMEOUT` that called the application busy or unresponsive. Measured on GTK3 and GTK4, it now ends at 2.05-2.21 s instead of 5.02 s. A control the app enables within about 1.6 s is still clicked, and a `--timeout-ms` other than the default waits the whole budget. A headless click, right-click, double-click, triple-click or keyboard input refused because the element has no semantic action now says that `--headed`, when pointer or focus input is acceptable, is the way to act on it, instead of "Waiting will not help". ([#178](https://github.com/gshost1/agent-desktop/pull/178))
* A ref refused with `AMBIGUOUS_TARGET` `identity_not_unique` no longer says the refmap came from an earlier version and to take a new snapshot. That was wrong for a fresh ref, and a new snapshot refuses the same twin again. The suggestion now says what the capture saw. For a menu entry from a whole-window snapshot (role `menuitem` or `menu`), it says to open the menu and act on the ref from `snapshot --surface menu`, which a GTK3 menu with two `Close` entries confirms resolves and clicks. A twin inside the open menu gets the keyboard or `--headed mouse-click --xy`. ([#178](https://github.com/gshost1/agent-desktop/pull/178))
