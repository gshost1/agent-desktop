### Bug Fixes

* **linux:** `launch` now waits up to `--timeout` for an app that is still starting instead of returning after 5 s with no window and a suggestion that blamed its toolkit. A GTK3 app that opens its window 8 s after the spawn now returns with its window at 8.8 s (was `ok` with no window at 5.5 s). It still ends after 5 s when the process maps an X11 window without joining the accessibility bus, or runs with `NO_AT_BRIDGE=1` or `GTK_A11Y=none`, and the suggestion names which case it saw. A process with no window yet is reported at the timeout with `agent-desktop wait --app <app> --window <title> --timeout 30000` as the next step. A forwarded launch of a running single-instance app that only changes its page (`launch gnome-control-center --arg datetime --activate`) now returns that app and its window in 7.9 s instead of `ACTION_FAILED` after 30 s. ([#PRNUM](https://github.com/gshost1/agent-desktop/pull/PRNUM))
