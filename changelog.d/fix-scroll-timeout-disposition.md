### Bug Fixes

* On macOS, a page scroll whose `kAXErrorCannotComplete` came from its messaging timeout keeps `APP_UNRESPONSIVE` with an uncertain disposition instead of `ACTION_NOT_SUPPORTED`, `not_delivered`, retry safe. The app may finish the scroll after the 250 ms settle window, and a bench fixture whose scroll area shows a pinned header first was reported not delivered in 5 of 5 runs although its rows had moved, so a retry scrolled twice. A refusal the app answers in time is still settled; SwiftUI answers one in 0-4 ms. The settle now reads the frames of the area's first four content children and of the first one's first four children, and one unchanged frame without a scroll bar no longer counts as proof. A reveal that stops because page steps kept moving the target past the viewport fails `ACTION_FAILED` with `kind: scroll_step_overshoot` instead of saying the scroll areas moved as far as they can.
